    Things to try

    • Low strings: play open E and A, then frets 1–5. Watch for the octave jumping up.
    • Scale: play a slow major scale. Every note should appear in the log once.
    • Repeated notes: pick the same note 4 times. It should log 4 times.
    • Legato: hammer-ons and pull-offs should log each new note.
    • Speed: play a phrase faster and faster until notes start to go missing.
    • Response time: the ms figure in the log is the time from your pluck until the note is accepted.
